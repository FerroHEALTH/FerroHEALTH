# Annex III 5: a copy of the information sheet

Revised 2026-10-26.

EHDS Annex III point 5 ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj)): "A copy of the
information sheet referred to in Article 38."

The information sheet is the [information sheet](information-sheet.md) of
this book, at the revision each release pins. Each release of FerroEHR and of
FerroBRIDGE attaches a copy of that revision to its GitHub release, with the
release's own facts appended under "This copy": the version of each product,
the release date and the versions of the standards.

EHDS Art. 38(3) allows the information to be entered into the EU database of
Art. 49 in place of supplying the sheet with the system. FerroEHR is not
registered there (registration follows a declaration of conformity), so the
sheet is supplied with each release.

State: available.
